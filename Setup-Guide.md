# Import
<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c17f5a0-15f3-4396-a277-a66638017fb3"> </p> 

Click on `Import` in [streamer.bot](https://streamer.bot/) and drag the [Slot-Machine-Game.sb](https://github.com/aaskjer/Slot-Machine-Game/releases) file into the window or copy paste the [import string](https://github.com/aaskjer/Slot-Machine-Game/blob/main/Import-String.md).

<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd761fe1-bc20-4d0e-be24-1b00dc4bc529"></p> 

Click on `Import` and `Yes` on both following popup messages.

## Enable Commands
<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c4d780df-3f55-4215-a485-4b23ff8263be"></p> 

Enable all commands in the `Commands` tab of streamer.bot.

# Setup
<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71ddb888-73fb-47bd-81eb-ec314d5d69f0"> </p> 

Right-Click on `Test` and click `Test Trigger` in the trigger section of `[HM] - Settings` to open the GUI of the game.
